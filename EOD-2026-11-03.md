## What was built

### 1. Mobile header and menu (client request)
Modelled on davidghiyam.com, as Mike asked.

- Up to 900px wide the header is **☰ | logo | Sign in**. The row of links under
  the logo is gone on mobile; desktop is unchanged
- ☰ opens a full-screen white menu with Mike's items, in his order: Log in,
  Membership, Rav Miz AI, About, Lectures, Events
- Shared by the Home, About and Let's Chat pages (`assets/site-common.css`)
- Works as a proper dialog: Esc closes it, keyboard focus stays inside, and it
  closes itself if the window is widened past mobile size
- "Sign in" turns into "Go to app" for members who are already signed in

### 2. Header stays pinned while scrolling (client follow-up)
The Home header now stays at the top at every screen size, as the About and
Let's Chat headers already did. The first pass covered phones only; it was
widened to desktop after feedback.

### 3. Hero video shows on mobile
On phones the video was stretched over the whole copy *and* the phone mockup,
over 1,200px tall from a 360×640 clip, under an 88% white overlay. Almost
nothing of it showed.

- It now fills the first screen and fades out behind the phone, under a
  lighter overlay; the headline gets a soft glow to stay readable
- Still frames (`hero-poster.jpg`, `hero-poster-portrait.jpg`) show when
  autoplay is blocked
- iPhones in Low Power Mode, which block autoplay, start it on the first tap

### 4. Footer social links
Linkme, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, in Mike's order, on Home, About and
Let's Chat.

### 5. Continue with Google (code done, setup not done yet)
"The login with Gmail does not work" had two causes: the button was hard-coded
as disabled, and the Google provider is switched off in Supabase.

- The button is now wired to Supabase's Google sign-in
- It checks that Google is enabled first. Until it is, users see *"Google
  sign-in is not available yet. Please continue with email."* instead of a raw
  Supabase error page
- Google sign-ups from the signup page continue to Stripe Checkout, the same as
  email sign-ups
- A cancelled or failed Google login returns to the login page with the reason
- `sql/006_oauth_profile_names.sql` — Google members get their real name on
  their profile instead of "Member", and names are capped at 80 characters so
  an over-long one cannot fail the sign-up — **not applied yet**

## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Signed-in members saw "Log in / Try free" on the live site.** The code
   looked for links written exactly as `login.html`, but Netlify rewrites them
   to `/login`. It worked locally and failed only in production. It now
   compares the resolved page path. Confirmed with the old code before fixing.

2. **The signup "I agree to the Terms…" line broke into fragments.** Each piece
   of the sentence was laid out as a separate column.

3. **The trial page's tab title still said "3-Day".** Now 7-Day.
