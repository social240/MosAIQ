4. Privacy of AI Conversations

Your conversations are private.

RavMizAI is designed so that each registered user can access their own conversation history through their authenticated account.

Other subscribers cannot view your private conversations through their accounts.

We do not publicly publish individual conversations.

We do not sell private conversation content to advertisers.

We do not use identifiable private conversations for targeted advertising.

4.1 Conversation Recordings

RavMizAI records and processes voice conversations to provide its AI calling functionality.

Voice recordings and transcripts may be stored and associated with the user’s account.

Recording and transcription enable:

  • AI conversation functionality.
  • Conversation history.
  • Continuity within supported sessions.
  • Usage measurement.
  • Technical reliability.

Users will receive appropriate notice of recording before or when initiating voice interactions, as required by applicable law.

4.2 Access to Conversations

Conversation histories are intended to be accessible only to the account holder through the ordinary user interface.

Authorized technical personnel or contracted service providers may process or access conversation information only when reasonably necessary for limited purposes, including:

  • Operating the AI service.
  • Resolving technical problems.
  • Investigating security incidents.
  • Preventing abuse.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

Such access should be restricted according to job responsibilities and appropriate confidentiality and security controls.

We do not provide routine access to individual user conversations to unrelated subscribers, advertisers, or the general public.
